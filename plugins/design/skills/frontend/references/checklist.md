# Review checklist

Go through every line. Mark each as passing, failing (with where and the fix) or not verified (with
why). Read the page's source; when the user allows a preview tool the environment already has, also
look at it at 1440, 1024, 820, 768, 390 and 320 px wide.

## First screen

- Says what is sold, for whom or where, with one hard fact (a number, a place, a price or a date).
- The hero fills it alone at every width; nothing of the next section shows; its main button is
  inside it.

## One section per screen

- Every section is one or two screens tall; its h2 comes first and is top-aligned.
- No neighbour's heading or picture shows in a section's screen; nothing sparse or half empty.

## Equal edges

- Every block sits on the `.wrap` lines or the grid's columns; nothing out to one side.
- No sideways scroll at any width.

## Visuals

- Every visual draws at 1440, 1024, 768 and 390; an effect that cannot run shows a still.
- No caption explains, apologises for or labels a picture; credits in one footer line.
- No placeholder, sample note or printed `.example` address.
- Missing client material sits in a finished-looking slot and is listed in the report.

## The right things for the kind

- Outside tech: no effect ground, print, scene or loop; only photo- or type-led moments.
- The subject itself shows: the product big and whole, in three sections at most, a new picture
  each; each asset the brief names in its slot; software shows its own UI in an app frame.
- Real photographs where they fit, none used twice; real icons in their own colours; flat grounds
  outside tech.

## Motion

- Every button, link, linked card and linked photograph visibly answers the pointer; nothing
  tilts.
- The hero enters once on load; one or two dealt moments.
- Nothing moves because the page scrolled, except a moment inside its own section (or a tech
  page's one big scene).
- Anything moving past 5s has a pause button.
- Under reduced motion everything rests and nothing stays hidden.

## Contacts

- Only what the brief gives; nothing invented; missing contacts listed in the report.

## Keyboard and head

- Every control has a name; `alt` on pictures; visible labels on fields; headings one level at a
  time; no `tabindex` above 0.
- Tab reaches a skip link first; the focus ring shows at once and never under the header; the menu
  button announces its state.
- `lang`, title, description and share tags are set.

## One system

- One button pair, one card style, one radius scale, one link look; two boxes deep at most.
- One heading scale, its faces loaded; text contrast of at least 4.5:1.

## Copy and links

- Headings name things; buttons are a verb and an object; at most two hero actions.
- One action at most twice on a screen; one fact, one wording.
- Assumed facts carry `data-assumed` and are in the report.
- Every link lands where its label says; forms send; a confirmation only after a real send; each
  error under its own field.
- No tell from `copy.md` used as a tic.

## Weight

- Images sized for their frame; no layout shift on load; no third-party script or CDN added.

## 820, 768, 390 and 320

- Sections on their screens; nothing lies on anything else or under a fixed bar.
- Every link and control outside running text is at least 44px tall.
- Nothing past the screen at 320.
- Splits stack text first; no row half empty; one column at 390, buttons full width, a full-screen
  menu.

## Your eyes

- It reads as a professional company's site; nothing tilted, staggered, cut or overlapping; the
  hero shows the work, not a mood.
